Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: 19 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 13 August 2026

1. Who We Are

Cric Captain is a cricket team management application designed for cricket captains and players to manage squads, schedule matches, plan bowling rotations, and coordinate availability.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: criccaptain.app@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

You provide
  • Account information — email address and password when you register
  • Profile information — your display name and role (captain or player)
  • Team data — team name, squad player names, and player roles
  • Match data — match details, playing XI, bowling plans, opponent notes, and availability responses
  • Privacy policy agreement — timestamp of when you accepted this policy
Collected automatically
  • Device push token — to deliver match notifications (only if you grant permission)
  • Crash and error reports — via Sentry, to diagnose and fix faults. These contain the technical details of the fault and your account identifier, so we can tell whether a problem affects one account or many. They do not contain your name, email address, or any team or match content.
  • Subscription status — via RevenueCat, to determine whether your account has an active subscription. We never see or receive your card details; payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google.
We do NOT collect
  • Location data
  • Contacts or phone book
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data is stored per user account and is only accessible by you and members of your team via invite code.

Your team and match data is held in Google's europe-north1 region, in Finland. Some of our processors are based outside the European Economic Area, or may access data from outside it for support purposes. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses under our data processing agreement with that provider.

Firebase Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, your device's push token is saved to your account. We use this to notify you of new matches scheduled by your captain. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings — revoking permission stops future notifications.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data. We only use the following processors:

  • Google Firebase — data storage and authentication
  • Expo — push token routing to Apple APNs / Google FCM
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting (technical fault details and your account identifier)
  • RevenueCat — subscription status (your account identifier and whether a subscription is active)
  • Apple and Google — subscription payments. They act as the seller and handle payment entirely; we never receive your card details.
  • Legal obligations — if required by applicable law

Each of these acts as a processor on our instructions under a data processing agreement. None of them are permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

8. Squad Members Added by a Captain

Cric Captain lets a captain build a squad by entering players' names, and optionally their email addresses in order to invite them. This means we may hold information about you that you did not give us yourself, and possibly before you have ever opened the app.

If a captain has added you to a squad: the information held is your name, your playing role, your availability for matches, and — if the captain invited you — your email address. It is visible to that captain and to other members of that team. It is not used for marketing, and it is never sold.

The captain who entered your details decides what is recorded about you and is responsible for having a proper reason to record it. We provide the app that stores it. If you want your details corrected or removed, you can ask your captain directly, or contact us at criccaptain.app@gmail.com and we will act on your request even if you do not have an account with us.

9. Children's Privacy

Cric Captain is not directed at children, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to register.

We recognise that club squads often include junior players, and that a captain may add a child's name to a squad under section 8. Captains are responsible for obtaining the consent of a parent or guardian before entering a child's personal details, and for entering no more than is needed to organise the team. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's information has been recorded without your agreement, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — update incorrect information via the app
  • Deletion — delete your account and all data from Profile › Personal details in the app, or ask us to do it
  • Portability — request your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interests, such as crash reporting
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data while a query is resolved
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notification permission at any time in your device settings, without affecting anything done beforehand

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at criccaptain.app@gmail.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you think we have handled your data improperly, you have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Ours is the Finnish Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi/en). We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.
