Quick answers

Questions captains ask

What is Cric Captain?

Cric Captain is a cricket team management app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Captains build the playing XI, place fielders on a visual pitch, check player availability and schedule matches and tournaments, then share the plan with the whole team.

Who is Cric Captain for?

It is built for club and school cricket captains who want less admin and more time on the pitch, and for the players who need to see the plan.

Is Cric Captain free?

Cric Captain is free to download. Captains get a 14-day free trial, and players can always join and use the app for free. An optional Pro subscription unlocks unlimited teams, templates and full match history. Current pricing is shown in the App Store and Google Play.

How do players join a team?

Ask your team's captain for a join code. Create an account in the app, enter the code when you sign up, and you are linked to the team automatically.

Can I save and reuse field setups?

Yes. Save field placements as templates and reuse them for different formats, or build a custom setup for a match or a bowler.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile, then Personal details, then tap Delete my account. You can also follow the steps on the account deletion page.
