Common questions

I forgot my password

On the login screen tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I join my team as a player?

Ask your team admin for an invite code, then choose Create an account in the app and enter the code during signup. You'll be linked to your team automatically.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Personal details → Delete account. Or see the account deletion page for step-by-step instructions. Deletion permanently removes your account and all data.
